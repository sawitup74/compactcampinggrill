Vegetarian Camp Cooking on the Grill

Meat-free camp meals: grilled veggies, halloumi, portobello burgers, and quesadillas.

Vegetarian camp meals can be just as satisfying as meat-based ones. The grill brings out the sweetness in vegetables and adds smoky flavor.

Grilled vegetables

Zucchini, bell peppers, onions, corn, mushrooms and eggplant all grill well. Cut thick, brush with oil, season, and grill over medium heat.

Halloumi skewers

Halloumi holds its shape on the grill. Skewer cubes with vegetables and grill until golden.

Portobello burgers

Marinate portobello caps in olive oil, garlic and balsamic. Grill until tender and serve on buns with cheese.

Quesadillas

Fill tortillas with cheese, beans and grilled vegetables. Toast on a griddle or grate.

Clean grill matters

Vegetarians and vegans especially appreciate a clean grill, since a public grate is coated in strangers' meat drippings. Your own grill solves that.

Questions campers ask

What vegetables grill best at camp?

Zucchini, peppers, onions, corn, mushrooms and eggplant.

Can I grill cheese?

Halloumi and similar grilling cheeses hold their shape.

Why bring my own grill as a vegetarian?

Public grates often carry meat residue from previous users.
