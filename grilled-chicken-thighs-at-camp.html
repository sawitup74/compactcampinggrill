Grilled Chicken Thighs at Camp

Juicy bone-in or boneless thighs over two-zone charcoal, with a simple rub and a thermometer check.

Chicken thighs are the most forgiving chicken for camp grilling. They stay juicy, take on smoke beautifully and are hard to overcook.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken thighs
  • Oil
  • Rub: 1 tsp each salt, paprika, garlic powder, brown sugar, and 1/2 tsp pepper

Prep

Mix the rub at home. At camp, pat thighs dry, oil, and season.

Cooking

  1. Set up a two-zone fire.
  2. Start skin side down on the cooler side.
  3. Cook covered with foil or a pan lid.
  4. Move to the hot side to crisp the skin.
  5. Cook to 165°F; many cooks prefer thighs a bit higher for tenderness.

Serving

With grilled corn, potato skewers or tortillas.

Food safety

Keep raw chicken sealed and at the bottom of the cooler. Wash hands and boards after handling.

Questions campers ask

How long do chicken thighs take on a camp grill?

About 20 to 30 minutes depending on size and heat.

What temperature should chicken thighs reach?

At least 165°F.

How do I keep raw chicken safe at camp?

Seal it and keep it at the bottom of a cold cooler.
