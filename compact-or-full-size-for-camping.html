Slot Grill Compact or Full Size: Which One for Your Camping Style?

Match the right Slot Grill to how you camp: solo, couples, families, kayaks, RVs and truck camping.

The Slot Grill comes in two sizes: Compact and Full Size. Both share the same tab-and-slot design, made from thick carbon steel in Texas. The Compact is simply a scaled-down Full Size, built at 80 percent. The choice depends on how you camp and how many people you feed.

Solo and couples

The Compact. It is sized for one to three people, light enough to carry from a parking area, and slides into a trunk, kayak or camp box.

Families

The Full Size gives about 58% more cooking area, enough for burgers for a family in one round or a skillet next to the meat.

Portable fire pit

Love campfires? Bring your own clean one.

The Slot Grill Compact burns charcoal or cooking wood in a raised steel box with a 2⅞" air gap underneath, so where fires are allowed it doubles as a tidy personal fire pit after supper. Set up by hand in 30 to 60 seconds, no tools.

See the Compact · $79.97

Kayak and canoe camping

The Compact, since it packs about an inch thick and is lighter.

RV and travel trailer

Either. The Compact fits RV closets easily; the Full Size fits pass-through bays and suits families.

Truck camping and hunting camps

The Full Size, with its 3⅝-inch air gap that helps minimize radiant heat to truck beds and tables.

Groups

Run two grills side by side, or a Full Size and a Compact together.

Both burn charcoal or cooking wood, so where fires are allowed, either can serve as a small fire pit. The Full Size makes a bigger fire for a larger group.

Which Slot Grill is better for camping?

The Compact suits solo campers, couples and paddlers; the Full Size suits families, groups and truck camping.

Can I add a griddle?

Yes, the Slot Grill Griddle (Plancha) add-on works with the Slot Grill system.
