Campfire Kabobs: Chicken, Beef and Veggie Skewers

Prep-at-home skewers that cook fast over charcoal, with marinades, timing and a no-foil cleanup.

Kabobs are the perfect camp food. You prep everything at home, they cook in minutes, and everyone can build their own. They also cook beautifully over the steady heat of a compact charcoal grill.

Ingredients (serves 3 to 4)

  • 1.5 lb chicken thighs or sirloin, cut into 1.25-inch cubes
  • 2 bell peppers, cut into squares
  • 1 red onion, cut into chunks
  • 1 zucchini, thick half-moons
  • Cherry tomatoes or mushrooms
  • Metal skewers

Marinade

  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp each salt, smoked paprika and cumin
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

At home

Mix the marinade, divide between two bags, and add meat to one and vegetables to the other. Freeze the meat bag flat; it doubles as ice and thaws by dinner on day one or two.

At camp

  1. Light a medium-hot fire and set up two zones.
  2. Thread meat and vegetables on separate skewers so each cooks for the right time.
  3. Grill meat over the hot side, turning every few minutes, about 10 to 12 minutes for chicken thighs or 6 to 8 for medium beef.
  4. Grill veggie skewers on the cooler side until tender with char.
  5. Check chicken reaches 165°F with an instant-read thermometer.

Serving ideas

  • Wrap in warm pita with yogurt sauce.
  • Serve over rice cooked on the grill.
  • Add a salad from the cooler.

Cleanup

Metal skewers wipe clean and pack flat. No foil to throw away, and nothing tossed in the fire ring.

Questions campers ask

Should I soak wooden skewers?

If you use wooden skewers, soaking helps keep them from burning. Metal skewers are reusable and avoid the issue.

How long do chicken kabobs take to cook?

About 10 to 12 minutes over medium-hot coals, to 165°F.

Can I freeze marinated meat for camping?

Yes. Freeze it flat; it acts as ice and thaws in the cooler.
