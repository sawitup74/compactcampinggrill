RV Cooking Outdoors: Keep the Heat and Smell Out of the Rig

Why cooking outside the camper is better, and how to set up an outdoor kitchen at an RV site.

Cooking inside an RV heats it up, fills it with smells and coats surfaces with grease. Cooking outside keeps your rig clean and cool, and it's more fun.

Outdoor kitchen layout

  • A folding table near the RV door
  • Your grill on a stable, non-combustible surface away from the rig and awning
  • A cooler or use of the fridge for cold storage
  • A wash tub

Safety

  • Never grill under an awning or slide-out.
  • Keep a fire extinguisher handy.
  • Keep the grill well away from propane connections.
  • Never use charcoal inside the RV or a screen room.

Menu ideas

Burgers, fajitas, grilled fish and pancakes on a griddle.

Questions campers ask

Should I cook inside or outside my RV?

Outside keeps the rig cooler and cleaner.

Where should I put a grill at an RV site?

On a stable, non-combustible surface away from the RV, awning and propane connections.

Can I use charcoal in a screen room?

No. Charcoal must be used in open air.
