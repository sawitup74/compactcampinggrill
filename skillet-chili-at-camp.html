One-Skillet Camp Chili

A hearty chili made in cast iron over charcoal, perfect for cool nights and leftovers.

On a cool night at camp, nothing beats a bowl of chili. Made in a cast iron skillet over a charcoal fire, it is warm, filling and easy.

Ingredients (serves 4)

  • 1 lb ground beef or sausage
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cans beans, drained
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper

At home

Dice the onion and pepper, and mix spices in a jar.

At camp

  1. Heat the skillet over medium coals.
  2. Brown the meat and drain excess fat into a can, never onto the ground.
  3. Add onion and pepper.
  4. Add beans, tomatoes and spices.
  5. Simmer 20 to 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Toppings

Cheese, sour cream, onions, hot sauce, corn chips.

Why cast iron

Leftovers

Chill quickly and reheat for breakfast with eggs, or for chili dogs.

Questions campers ask

Can I make chili on a camping grill?

Yes, in a cast iron skillet or pot over medium charcoal heat.

What do I do with grease at camp?

Pour it into a can, let it solidify, and pack it out.

How long does camp chili take?

About 30 to 40 minutes.
