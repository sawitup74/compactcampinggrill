Griddle Pancakes and Bacon for a Camp Crowd

Fluffy pancakes and crisp bacon on a carbon steel griddle over charcoal, with a shaker-bottle batter.

Pancakes at camp are a tradition worth keeping. With a carbon steel griddle over a small charcoal fire, they cook evenly and brown beautifully.

Gear

A flat griddle plate makes pancakes easy. The Slot Grill Griddle (Plancha) add-on is built from the same thick carbon steel as the grills and comes in half or full griddle configurations. A half griddle leaves room for bacon on the grate next to it.

Shaker-bottle batter

Measure your favorite dry pancake mix or homemade dry blend into a large shaker bottle at home. At camp, add water (or milk and an egg) and shake. No bowl to wash.

Camp griddle

Pancakes, eggs and smash burgers at camp

The Slot Grill Griddle (Plancha) add-on is built from the same thick carbon steel, comes in half or full griddle configurations, and flat-stacks with your Slot Grill. Grill not included.

See the griddle add-on

Bacon first

  1. Light a medium fire.
  2. Lay bacon on the griddle and cook until crisp, flipping occasionally.
  3. Move bacon to a plate and leave a thin film of fat on the griddle.

Pancakes

  1. Test heat with a few drops of water; they should dance and evaporate.
  2. Pour batter into small rounds.
  3. Flip when bubbles appear and edges look set.
  4. Cook another minute or two.

Heat control

Small fires are easier. If pancakes brown too fast, spread coals out or move the griddle off the hottest spot.

Toppings

Syrup, butter, berries, chocolate chips, peanut butter or bananas.

Cleanup

Scrape the griddle while warm, wipe with a paper towel, and re-oil lightly.

Questions campers ask

How do I make pancakes at camp?

Use a shaker bottle for batter and a preheated griddle over medium heat.
